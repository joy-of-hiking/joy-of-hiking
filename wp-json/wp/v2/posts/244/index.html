In Late August 2022, we crossed Hardangervidda, a mountain plateau in central southern Norway. During our 11-day adventure, we wandered with our tents, saw almost no one but a bunch of lemmings and sheep, spontaneously joined a bridge-building group, and made our way from Haukeliseter, at the southern edge, to Finse, the northern end of the national park, just after passing by one of Norway\u2019s largest glaciers, Hardangerj\u00f8kulen. This is the story of our hike, where I also share practical tips on hiking in Hardangervidda, including finding safe drinking water, camping in Norway, and using the DNT huts.<\/p>\n\n\n

Getting to Haukeliseter, the starting point<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

To get to Norway from Germany, the most common option is to fly to Oslo and get a bus from there to the start of the trail. However, we wanted to make our traveling as eco-friendly as possible and had the time for a long journey. So, our two-day adventure began, with one train ride and two bus trips ahead of us. As there were direct buses from northern Germany to Oslo, we first took a train to L\u00fcbeck, made a half-day stop to visit some friends, and then continued our journey with a 14-hour night bus to Oslo.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Once arrived at the bus station in Oslo, we took another bus that brought us directly to the start of the trail in Haukeliseter, which took about 5 hours. Just after early afternoon, we arrived at the mountain lodge Haukeliseter. There, we picked up a key that gives access to all the mountain huts along the route, which we could return at the end of our hike in Finse. Although we planned to camp, having the key meant we could get supplies or a warm, dry place to sleep if needed.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Day 1 – Haukeliseter to \u00c5lmannvegen, 3 km<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

As we were able to start the trail only after 5 pm, we decided to make a short day. After a steep 150-meter climb, we found a spot to pitch our tent with soft ground and not so far away from the river. We cooked the heaviest meal we had with ourselves to lighten our backpacks (instant spinach lentil curry) and made ourselves comfortable in our sleeping bags, ready to rest from the long trip and excited for the coming days.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
